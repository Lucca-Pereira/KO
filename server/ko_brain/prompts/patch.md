Decide whether the user's last message asked for a concrete change to the recipe.

A change means: swapping or removing an ingredient, scaling it, changing a technique or step,
or adapting it (vegan, gluten-free, no oven, air fryer).

A question is not a change: "what can I use instead of cream?" is asking, "make it dairy-free"
is asking for a change.

If they asked for a change, return the complete edited recipe, keeping everything they did not
ask you to touch exactly as it was. If they did not, return the recipe completely unchanged and
leave `summary` empty.

Apply the change to the *whole* recipe, not just the ingredient that prompted it. Making a
recipe dairy-free means the butter and the parmesan too, not only the cream. Check every
ingredient and every step against what was asked before you answer.
